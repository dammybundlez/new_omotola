Reel 3

The Woman
Behind the Legend

Born on February 7, 1978, in Lagos, Omotola Jalade Ekeinde entered an industry that would never be the same.

Her breakout in Mortal Inheritance (1996) announced a talent so commanding it would reshape Nollywood's identity. Over the next three decades, she became more than an actress—a cultural architect, humanitarian, and the face of African cinema worldwide.

“She carried African narratives beyond borders, redefining what it means to be a global artist from the continent.”

— • —

The Journey

From Lagos to the global stage — a timeline of milestones

  1. 1996

    The Breakthrough

    Stars in Mortal Inheritance. Wins Best Actress Overall at THEMA 1997.

  2. 2005

    Humanitarian Pivot

    Appointed UN World Food Programme Goodwill Ambassador. Launches debut album GBA.

  3. 2010

    Global Box Office

    Co-stars in Ije: The Journey, the highest-grossing Nollywood film at the time.

  4. 2012

    Reality TV Pioneer

    Launches Nigeria's first reality series, Omotola: The Real Me.

  5. 2013

    TIME 100

    First Nigerian actress named among TIME Magazine's 100 Most Influential People.

  6. 2014

    National Honor

    Awarded Member of the Order of the Federal Republic (MFR), one of Nigeria's highest honors.

  7. 2018

    The Academy

    Invited to join the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences as a voting member.

  8. 2025

    Three Decades

    Celebrates 30 years in entertainment, leading Nollywood's global integration via Redhot Concepts.
