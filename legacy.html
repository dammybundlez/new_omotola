Years of Impact

Delve into the extraordinary journey of Omotola Jalade Ekeinde, a true cinematic icon of Africa and a trailblazer in cultural advocacy. From her groundbreaking performances that have captivated audiences worldwide to her significant contributions to humanitarian efforts, Omotola's story is one of resilience and inspiration. She has not only redefined the landscape of African cinema but has also used her platform to effect positive change in communities across the globe.

A Legacy Etched in Time

Omotola Jalade Ekeinde's illustrious career spans over three decades, marked by groundbreaking achievements and a profound impact on the global entertainment landscape. A pioneering figure in Nollywood, she has redefined African cinema and inspired artists worldwide.

Early Life & Education

Omotola Jalade Ekeinde was born on 7 February 1978 in Lagos State to Oluwatoyin Jalade (née Amori Oguntade) and Oluwashola Jalade. She has two younger brothers, Tayo and Bolaji Jalade. Omotola attended Chrisland School, Oxford Children School, and Command Secondary School, Kaduna. She later studied Estate Management at Yaba College of Technology (1996–2004), originally planning a career in business management before pursuing modeling and acting.

Career Milestones

Omotola debuted in film in 1993, gaining Nollywood recognition with Mortal Inheritance. She has featured in over 300 films and earned a Doctorate in Arts from Igbinedon University, Okada in 2019. In 1996, she married Captain Matthew Ekeinde, with whom she has four children. Omotola became the first African celebrity to receive over one million Facebook likes and was listed among CNN Travel’s 12 Sexiest Accents in 2012. Her global recognition peaked when she appeared in Time Magazine’s 100 Most Influential People in 2013.

Honors & Cultural Impact

Omotola was honored as a Member of the Order of the Federal Republic (MFR) by the Nigerian government in 2014 for her contributions to cinema. She launched Africa’s first celebrity reality show, Omotola: The Real Me, on Africa Magic Entertainment. Her work continues to elevate Nollywood on the global stage, inspiring generations of artists and cultural ambassadors.

Popularly known as Omo Sexy, Omotola Jalade-Ekeinde has long been admired as one of the most iconic, curvaceous, and captivating actresses in the industry. Her screen presence and emotional depth have earned her critical acclaim and numerous award nominations across Nollywood’s most defining films. Performances in landmark titles such as Ties That Bind, Alter Ego, and Mortal Inheritance secured her multiple honors including Actress of the Year, Best Actress, and Best Actress Overall. Across her career, she has accumulated an extraordinary body of recognitions for both performance excellence and cultural influence. Beyond cinema, Omotola’s impact extends into humanitarian work through the Omotola Youth Empowerment Project (OYEP). One of its most poignant initiatives is the 20 Widows Makeover, created in honor of her late mother, who lived as a widow for a decade. The initiative received national recognition with a nomination for Best Charity/Cause Connected Event at the Nigerian Event Awards in 2012—cementing OYEP as both a social and cultural force. Today, Omotola stands as a United Nations World Food Programme Ambassador, an advocate for the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), and an active Amnesty International campaigner—bridging art, activism, and global responsibility.

Global Impact: The Queen of Nollywood

Omotola's career extends far beyond acting; she is a global brand ambassador and a voice for human rights and youth empowerment.

The Activist & Philanthropist

  • United Nations World Food Programme

    Serving as a Goodwill Ambassador since 2005, she has undertaken missions to Sierra Leone and Liberia to raise awareness for hunger and maternal health.

  • OYEP (Omotola Youth Empowerment Programme)

    Founded to empower youth through skill acquisition, conventions, and social advocacy.

  • Amnesty International

    Active involvement in campaigns advocating for human dignity, gender equality, and corporate accountability in the Niger Delta.
