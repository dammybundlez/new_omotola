Who We Are

About Redhot Concepts

The Architects of African Cinematic Structure

Redhot Concepts is a premium African production studio founded on the core philosophy that world-class talent demands world-class infrastructure.

We are more than a production house; we are an ecosystem builder dedicated to professionalizing the entertainment industry across Africa and exporting authentic, high-impact stories to the global stage.

Our Story — The Need for Ownership

Founded by Omotola Jalade Ekeinde, MFR, the genesis of Redhot Concepts began with a vision that extended beyond the screen.

After years of defining the face of Nollywood, Omotola recognized that an industry built on raw talent alone could not sustain its future. The studio was created in the mid-2000s out of a necessity to establish systems, financial integrity, and ownership—the missing pillars required to transform Nollywood into a globally competitive creative economy.

Today, Redhot Concepts anchors a thriving ecosystem of brands—including OYEP and TEFFEST—cultivating the next generation of African excellence.

Vision

To be the leading Pan-African studio creating content that transcends borders, builds scalable business models for African creatives, and drives the global cultural narrative.

Core Values

  • Excellence — Demanding world-class standards in every aspect of production and execution.
  • Authenticity — Telling humane, homegrown stories that retain Nigeria’s heart while engaging a global audience.
  • Structure & Professionalism — Championing fair labor advocacy, documentation, and long-term stability.
The Production Philosophy

African Heart.
Global Standard.

Our philosophy is centered on the intersection of creativity and commerce. We believe that cinematic quality is non-negotiable. Every Redhot Concepts project—from feature films like Mother’s Love to documentary series and commercial ventures—is executed with a commitment to high-impact visuals, powerful direction, and narratives that explore the depth of the human condition.

  1. Principle 01 Centers African Agency

    We control our narratives, ensuring they are told with integrity and free from external compromise.

  2. Principle 02 Prioritizes the Audience

    We create projects that are not only artful but designed to perform commercially on modern streaming and theatrical platforms.

  3. Principle 03 Invests in the Future

    Every production builds capacity, formalizes best practices, and raises industry standards.

  4. Principle 04 Structure & Professionalism

    Championing fair labor advocacy, proper documentation, and long-term financial stability for all practitioners.
