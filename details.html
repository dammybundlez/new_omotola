The Story

A coming-of-age tale of identity, sacrifice, and unbreakable bonds

Film Synopsis

Mother's Love is a powerful coming-of-age story inspired by real emotional truths. At its heart is a young woman's journey from comfort to self-discovery, set against the backdrop of Nigeria's National Youth Service Corps.

As she enters unfamiliar territory — geographically, socially, and emotionally — she begins to understand her mother's sacrifices, the meaning of service, and the strength of her own identity.

Blending emotional depth with cultural authenticity, Mother's Love explores themes of female empowerment, resilience, and transformation — creating a story that is both deeply Nigerian and universally human.

A story of identity, sacrifice, and the unbreakable bond between mother and child.
Director's Vision

Omotola Jalade-Ekeinde

Director's Statement

Mother's Love is more than a film to me, it's a deeply personal journey. As my directorial debut, it draws inspiration from real experiences, not just my own, but from the many untold stories of young Nigerian women finding their place in a complex world.

I wanted to tell a coming-of-age story that challenges privilege, confronts identity and ultimately celebrates the quiet strength of women redefining themselves on their own terms.

Set against the vibrant, layered backdrop of modern Nigeria, Mother's Love captures both the beauty and the friction of growth, how we wrestle with where we come from in order to discover who we truly are.

This film is performance-led, emotionally grounded, and socially aware. It speaks to universal themes of family, resilience, and empowerment while offering a lens into contemporary Nigerian life rarely seen on screen.

I believe authentic and bold stories like this deserve a global platform, and I'm honoured to share it with audiences ready to embrace something both intimate and impactful.

– Omotola Jalade-Ekeinde

Co-Writer & Director, Producer, Mother's Love

Behind the Production

Co-Producer's Statement

Mother's Love is a heartfelt exploration of the strength, sacrifice and silent resilience that define motherhood. Co-producing this film alongside Omotola Jalade Ekeinde was a powerful experience, as we sought to tell a story that honours the everyday heroism of women who love fiercely, even in the face of multiple challenges.

This film is a tribute to the mothers whose stories often go untold. With sincerity and purpose, we aimed to create a narrative that not only moves audiences but also sparks meaningful reflection on the depth of a mother's love.

We are proud to share this story with the world.

By Tyna Obahiagbon

Co-Producer

Behind the Scenes

Production Notes

Dive into the meticulous planning, creative choices, and challenges that brought Mother's Love to life.

Script & Storyboarding

Every scene was meticulously storyboarded to capture the emotional journey of our protagonist. Special attention was given to the NYSC sequence to maintain authenticity.

Cinematography

The film employs a rich visual language with warm tones, natural lighting, and dynamic camera angles to immerse viewers in the story's emotional depth.

Locations & Set Design

Shooting across iconic Nigerian landscapes, every set was designed to reflect both cultural authenticity and cinematic grandeur, creating an immersive experience.

Casting & Performance

The cast underwent extensive workshops to capture the nuances of their characters, ensuring performances that resonate with authenticity and emotion.

Music & Sound

Original scores and carefully curated soundscapes were used to amplify emotions, from intimate family moments to dramatic turning points.

Editing & Post Production

Post-production emphasized narrative pacing and visual continuity, combining color grading, sound design, and subtle visual effects to elevate storytelling.
